Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my data really private?
A: Yes. By default, 100% of your financial data stays encrypted in your browser using AES-256 encryption. If you choose to enable cloud backup, your data is encrypted with your password before it leaves your browser — we never see your unencrypted data. You have complete control either way.
Q: How is the AI advisor different from other tools?
A: Most tools just show you charts. Our AI advisor (powered by Claude) can answer ANY question about your retirement plan in plain English. It's like having a financial advisor available 24/7, included free.
Q: Do I need to be a financial expert to use this?
A: No! That's the point. The AI explains everything in simple terms. If you see something confusing, just click the "?" button or ask the AI advisor.
Q: What if I need help from a human advisor?
A: We provide export features so you can share your plan with your financial advisor, CPA, or family. We complement professional advice, not replace it.
Q: How does cloud backup work?
A: When you enable cloud backup, your plan is encrypted with your password using AES-256 encryption before leaving your browser. We store only the encrypted blob — we cannot read your data. You can access your plan from any device by signing in with your email and password.
Q: How long is it free?
A: All features are completely free during our beta period. Founding Members who join during beta will get permanent discounts (20% off forever) when we introduce paid plans.
Q: Can I trust the calculations?
A: All calculations are based on current IRS rules, SSA guidelines, and standard financial planning practices. However, this is an educational tool - always consult a licensed professional for personalized advice.
